# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Overview

OctoAcme uses a customer-first, iterative, data-informed project lifecycle to deliver small, testable increments that create measurable value. Projects emphasize clear ownership, defined outcomes, regular communication, active risk tracking, integrated quality checks, controlled releases, and continuous improvement through retrospectives and follow-up actions.

## Project Management Processes

- **Initiation and stakeholder alignment:** Validate the business need, define SMART goals and success metrics, identify stakeholders, capture early risks and dependencies, and confirm whether the initiative should move into planning.
- **Planning and backlog development:** Convert approved work into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, owners, dependencies, a Definition of Done, milestones, and a release plan.
- **Execution and progress tracking:** Manage work through a visible project board, use small pull requests linked to issues, run CI checks, track delivery metrics, and escalate blockers through the defined path.
- **Risk management and communication:** Maintain a risk register with owners and mitigation plans, review risks during weekly syncs, and provide weekly or milestone-based updates from a single source of truth.
- **Release and deployment:** Confirm acceptance criteria, CI, security scans, release notes, rollback plans, staging smoke tests, production deployment, post-deploy verification, and stakeholder announcements.
- **Retrospectives and continuous improvement:** After sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents, capture learnings, assign action items with owners and due dates, and track improvements in the backlog.

## Roles and Responsibilities

- **Project Managers:** Coordinate delivery plans, schedules, risks, dependencies, resources, meetings, documentation, status reporting, and stakeholder communication.
- **Product Managers:** Define customer and business outcomes, success metrics, product vision, roadmap priorities, backlog order, acceptance criteria, and trade-off decisions.
- **Developers:** Design, build, test, document, and deliver features while contributing to estimates, technical reviews, code reviews, and risk mitigation.
- **QA/Testing:** Validate quality and acceptance criteria through unit, integration, smoke, security, and manual testing practices where appropriate.
- **Stakeholders:** Provide input, align on priorities, approve decisions, review milestone progress, and support escalation or release communications when needed.
